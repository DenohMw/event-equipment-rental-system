Help Center

Here are a few quick answers about using EventEquip. If you need help with a particular booking or account, contact our support team using the details below.

How do I find equipment?

Open Browse Equipment to explore listings. Check the item details, location, price, and availability for your event dates before booking.

How do I make a booking?

Choose an item, enter your event dates and requested quantity, then follow the booking steps. Review the displayed delivery details, total price, and rental terms before you confirm.

How do I pay?

Use the payment option shown during checkout. EventEquip supports M-Pesa payments where available. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time security codes with anyone.

How can I list equipment?

Register as an owner using List Your Equipment, then follow the prompts to add your listings.

Need help with a booking or payment?

When you contact us, include your account email and booking reference if you have one. Please do not send passwords, M-Pesa PINs, or one-time codes.

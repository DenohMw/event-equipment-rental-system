Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

This policy explains how EventEquip handles personal information when you use our event equipment marketplace. It should be read with our Terms and Conditions.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use EventEquip, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, profile details, owner or business verification information, equipment listings, booking and delivery details, reviews, and messages. We may also receive payment references and transaction status from payment providers; we do not ask for your M-Pesa PIN or one-time security codes.

Our systems may record technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as requests made to the site, device or browser details, and security or error logs.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts and provide marketplace features.
  • Show listings, process booking requests, coordinate rentals, and communicate with customers and owners.
  • Support payment processing, record transaction status, and address payment questions.
  • Review owner or listing information, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep the platform secure.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

Information relevant to a booking may be shared with the customer or owner involved so they can coordinate the rental. We may share information with service providers that help host, maintain, secure, or operate EventEquip, including payment services where a transaction requires them. We may also disclose information when required by law or needed to protect users, the platform, or our legal rights.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect personal information. Information is kept for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, handle bookings and disputes, meet legal requirements, and protect the platform. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices and requests

You can ask us to access or correct personal information associated with your account, or request deletion where applicable. Some information may need to be retained to complete a booking, resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

6. Children

EventEquip is intended for people who can legally enter into rental and account agreements. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The current version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact EventEquip Kenya at eventequipkenya@gmail.com or +254 726 962 004.

This policy is intended to be read consistently with applicable Kenyan data protection law, including the Data Protection Act, 2019. It is not legal advice.

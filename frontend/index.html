How EventEquip works

Four steps from finding equipment to setting up your event.

1. Search

Browse equipment by category, location, price, and availability on your event dates.

2. Book

Choose your dates and quantity. The system checks real-time availability instantly.

3. Pay

Complete your booking with M-Pesa. Receive instant confirmation and a receipt.

4. Receive

Equipment is delivered to your venue on schedule. Setup and teardown handled by the owner.
